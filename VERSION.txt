WHAT WAS ACTUALLY BROKEN (found by tracing the pipeline, not by re-reading
claims):

1. LEGACY-PATH DISCONNECT (the #1 architecture bug class: "policy exists but
   the candidate never reaches the scheduler"): with [Scheduler] Enabled=0
   and Intelligence ON, TrySendLegacy iterated DecisionEngine.EnabledOrder,
   which did NOT contain the companion-only slots. Solo mode silently lost
   every self-sustain press on that path, even though the loop contained an
   explicit "companion-only slots are never allowed to fire WITHOUT
   intelligence" check whose intent was the opposite. FIX: EnabledOrder now
   inserts SelfHeal (before Main) and Mobility (after Defensive) without
   reordering the six MaxDps slots; the fallback array (intelligence OFF)
   stays byte-identical.
2. ABILITY-SPECIFIC SELF-HEAL GATES WERE INCOMPLETE: the SelfHeal branch
   ignored the ability's own `neverAutomatic` veto, its curated
   `useBelowHpPct` ceiling, and its target/range preconditions. FIX:
   never-automatic holds (raid/channel heals stay manual even in Solo),
   `useBelowHpPct` conserves above the ability's own threshold (e.g. Death
   Pact below 50%, Lay on Hands below 40%), `RequiresTarget` holds without a
   target, and a confirmed out-of-range melee heal SKIPs instead of burning
   a press + retry window.
3. SLOT-8 RANGE WAS NOT PROBED: the bridge's RANGE_SLOTS skipped the
   SelfHeal slot, so a melee heal (Impending Victory, Death Strike) could be
   scheduled while the target was out of melee. FIX: slot 8 joins the range
   tri-state probe (R31 high bits), and the policy consumes it.
4. UNBOUND TALENT COULD BLANK THE SLOT: ExtraSpellID returned the first
   READY entry, then WriteSlot dropped it if no keybind resolved - so a
   ready-but-unbound talent shadowed a bound alternative and left the slot
   empty. FIX: the getter now requires a resolvable keybind too.
5. TELEMETRY CROSS-CONTAMINATION: TrySendScheduled left `_telemetryDecision`
   / `_telemetryContext` from an earlier legacy tick; a mid-session
   scheduler toggle would record a stale decision against fresh candidates
   and replay as a mismatch. FIX: the scheduler path clears them (its
   explainability record is the plan).
